# Axomiya AI Foundry

### Open AI infrastructure for the Assamese language.

Axomiya AI Foundry is a community-driven, open-source initiative by **Axomiya IT Labs** focused on building language resources, datasets, tools, and research infrastructure for Assamese AI.

We are working to make high-quality Assamese language resources more accessible to researchers, developers, educators, students, institutions, and open-source communities.

---

## Why We Exist

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life—from search and education to accessibility, communication, research, and public services.

Modern foundation models such as GPT, Gemini, Claude, Llama, Mistral, Sarvam AI, and others are expanding multilingual AI capabilities. But strong AI ecosystems also depend on the availability of high-quality language resources.

For Assamese, openly available and reusable AI-oriented resources remain comparatively limited.

We believe the community can help build that missing layer.

Axomiya AI Foundry focuses on the **language infrastructure beneath AI applications**: structured datasets, speech resources, evaluation resources, documentation, tooling, and open research infrastructure.

We are not building another chatbot.

We are building resources that others can build with.

---

## What We're Building

### 🗂️ AxomiyaKosh

An open structured Assamese lexical resource designed to support language research, search, retrieval, educational tools, RAG systems, and other language technologies.

**Status:** In development

### 🎙️ AxomiyaVoice

A community-built Assamese speech resource containing recordings and verified transcriptions for speech technology research.

**Status:** In development

## Our Mission

> **Build open, reliable, and reusable language infrastructure that helps Assamese thrive in the age of artificial intelligence.**

We believe Assamese AI should not depend entirely on closed resources.

By building openly and collaboratively, we can create resources that students, researchers, developers, institutions, startups, and future generations can access and improve.

## Open by Design

We believe language infrastructure benefits from openness.

Our work emphasizes:

* Open development
* Transparent dataset versions
